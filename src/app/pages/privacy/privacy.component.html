Privacy

Last updated September 2026.

This is my personal portfolio. There are no accounts, no forms, and nothing to sign up for. The only thing that collects anything about your visit is Google Analytics.

Google Analytics

Every page on this site loads Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-B0Y95T7EJM). It sets cookies in your browser and sends Google a record of the pages you view. What I look at is which projects people open, roughly where visitors come from (country or region, not a street address), which site or search sent them here, and whether they are on a phone or a desktop.

I use that to work out which work is worth showing and what to fix. I do not try to identify individual visitors, and I do not sell or share the data. Google processes it under its own terms, described in Google's privacy policy.

What this site does not do

  • No advertising, retargeting, or marketing pixels.
  • No contact form, newsletter, or account, so there is nothing for me to store.
  • No selling or sharing of anything collected here.

Opting out

Blocking cookies for this site in your browser, or using Google's opt-out add-on, stops the analytics. The site works exactly the same either way.
